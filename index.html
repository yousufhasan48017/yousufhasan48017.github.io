Profile

A supply chain view of software

I came into IT through supply chain management and data science, which is why I tend to read a system as a chain of handoffs rather than a set of screens. Where a process breaks is usually where one team's output stops being another team's usable input.

Day to day that means running requirement-gathering sessions, writing the BRDs and FSDs that come out of them, prototyping the flow in Figma so that stakeholders and developers are arguing about the same picture, and keeping HMIS operational issues from turning into stalled clinical workflows.

Alongside that I build with AI agents and automation — mostly n8n workflows wired to generative AI APIs, aimed at the document-heavy parts of analysis work that nobody enjoys doing by hand.
